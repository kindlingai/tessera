# What-clause label

"What a launch feeds", "What the kernel needs", "What one pass shares". The
comment names a question, and the reader must work out the thing it labels.

Say what the item is, with the noun first:

    /// What a launch feeds before its picks.
    /// Inputs a launch feeds before its picks.

    /// What the kernel needs: device addresses and the round.
    /// The kernel's arguments: device addresses and the round.

"What" without a noun and verb after it ("What to favor when lowering") is a
different shape, and the rule leaves it.
