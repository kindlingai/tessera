# Paired heading

A heading that joins two generic section names pads one idea into a pair:
"Features and Benefits", "Installation and Setup", "Challenges and Future
Directions". The Wikipedia signs-of-AI-writing list names "Awards and
recognition" as the typical case. Keep the name that fits the section, or
name the thing itself: "Known limits" for "Challenges and Future
Directions".

A heading that names two real things covered together is fine: "Input and
output", "Errors and panics", "Build and test".

The detector flags a pair of generic section names. The Jev question compares a
padded pair with a real one.
