# Unfalsifiable claims

Every claim must be checkable by a reader with the code in front of them.
Families that fail this test:

- Puffery. "robust", "elegant", "powerful" name feelings. Quantify or delete.
- Vague attribution. "best practice", "industry standard" borrow authority
  from nobody in particular. Name the spec, the benchmark, or the incident.
- Management jargon. "low-hanging fruit" means "cheap, and I have not
  measured it". These phrases exist to make a claim unfalsifiable or to hide
  who decided what.

Some detectors here are technical words that are also management filler:
`pivot`, `bandwidth`, `ping`, `cadence`. They fire only in comments and prose.
